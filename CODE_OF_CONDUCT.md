# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in LUMOS AI welcoming for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Standards

- Be kind, empathetic, and constructive
- Respect differing viewpoints
- Give and accept feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy toward other community members

Unacceptable: harassment, trolling, insulting, doxxing, or other unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement

Report to maintainers via GitHub or security@lumos.ai (see SECURITY.md). All complaints reviewed promptly and fairly, with confidentiality. Enforcement may include warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban.

## Scope

Applies in all community spaces (repo, issues, PRs, discussions, Discord, events) and when representing the community.
